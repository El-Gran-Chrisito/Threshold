Privacy Policy

Last updated: [date]

What stays on your device

Your designs, settings, branding, license key and trial start date are stored in your browser's local storage. We do not receive them unless you send them to us, or you use design sync (below).

What we receive when you buy

Payments are handled by [Stripe]. We receive your name, email address, the plan you bought and the payment status. We do not receive or store your card details. We use this to issue and renew your license and to answer support requests.

License server

When you return from checkout or your license renews, your browser sends the checkout reference or license key to our license server. The server checks it with our payment provider and returns a license key.

We email your license key to the address you paid with, through [Resend], so you can use it on other devices. If you ask us to email a lost key, the server looks up purchases for that address with our payment provider and sends the key only to that address. We keep a short-lived counter of these requests to prevent abuse.

Design sync (Pro and Studio)

On paid plans, the designs you save are also stored on our license server, linked to your license, so they open on every device where you enter your key. Deleting a design in Project deletes it from the server. Ask us at [support email] to delete all of them.

Store links

"Find it" links on the shopping list open a store's search page with the item's name. The store then receives what any visitor's browser sends. [Some links are affiliate links: if you buy, the store may pay us a commission, at no cost to you.]
